MySQL · SQL

E-Commerce Customer Churn Analysis

Business question
Why are customers leaving, and which segments are most at risk?
Approach
Cleaned 5,630 records in SQL by removing outliers, imputing 1,850+ missing values and standardizing categories. Built churn, complaint and warehouse-distance segments with CASE logic, then answered 18 business questions using joins, subqueries and aggregations.
Outcome
Overall churn was 16.8%. Customers who filed a complaint churned at 31.7% against 10.9% for those who did not, and 53.6% of all churned customers had raised a complaint, which makes complaint handling a clear retention lever. Tier 3 cities showed the highest churn (21.4% against 14.5% in Tier 1).
